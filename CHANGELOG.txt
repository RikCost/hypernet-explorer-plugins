- a cave system runs under the whole world and inside the mountains: three levels of branching passages, chambers and shafts
- the caves are walkable and diggable by the light you carry, never breaking the surface or flooding
- towns are built of buildings, not huts: a city square carries a dozen at 460 square metres, three or four rooms a floor and stairs, a village 270

- houses are bigger, with pitched roofs, eaves and ceilings
- a quarter of village houses and lower city floors are shops, roofed blue, with somebody behind the counter showing that shop's stock
- farms, lone houses and abandoned structures are scattered through open country
- parked cars stopped being left in the middle of crossroads
- the omega tower stands on its own square: six world squares of black stone, 750 metres across and three kilometres up on 29 gold banded setbacks, burning gold at night, drawn past four kilometres so it stays on the horizon
- the world map travel menu offers a free walk into the 3d world on foot, through real streets, shops, parks and fields
- every house and skyscraper is a shell whose multi-floor interior builds itself as you come near, walkable and climbable
- the hand made towns' squares are walls you are asked whether to enter, through the door on the side you came at
- directional citizens walk the pavements to talk to or empathize with, and the party and pet follow you
- creatures roam by the movement personality their own notes give them, with sight arcs, telegraphed alerts and chases that break at their leash
- birds really fly and swoop, and aquatic fauna lives in the sea, rivers and lakes, met by diving to it
- walking into a creature or landing a swing or a shot on it opens the fight on the spot, on the ground you met it on, with the 3d enemies and the hud over the live world
- creatures are measured and scaled to a height of their own, and everybody stands eye to eye because a sprite is cut to the figure drawn in it
- creatures remember their wounds and severed limbs between fights and leave a lootable body
- a fight no longer draws the flat shadow ellipse across whatever hill the monster is standing on
- the party can swim and free dive, and a leader who knows the fly skill takes off by double tapping jump
- head bob, footsteps, crouching, wall runs and a recoil launch on the vector gun and bubba's shotgun
- you dig with your own weapon, which swings or fires, stronger weapons taking fewer blows and ranged ones breaking blocks across a field
- broken blocks go on a voxel quick bar the wheel or l1/r1 runs, remembered in the world and refusing new kinds once full
- every cube dug and tree felled is written into the world's own folder, not into whichever savegame was written afterwards
- flat scenery is solid: you walk round a tree, ride over a rock, fell a tree at speed, and the action key opens the same fell, mine and forage menu
- vehicles drive up two block steps like a ramp
- every menu opens over the world instead of underneath it, the world held still and the mouse handed back, while anything you cannot click stays where it was
- a line of dialogue or a choice list is drawn over the world instead of blanking back to the 2d map
- a split-screen session carries in: the canvas is cut in two and the world drawn twice a frame, each player a body in the other's half, back to one view when they are close or both aboard
- every change of equipment or leader shows at once in the weapon in hand and the followers at heel
- the menu key opens on the first press, back opens the party menu on foot or the vehicle options at the wheel, and t ends the walk
- the control legend folds away behind h, with the toasts and the item quick bar lifted over the world
- the minimap cycles zoom, whole world, a draggable full map and off, with the arrow turning as you do
- repainted in a blocky 2009 palette: no tone curve, flat fill light, #78a7ff overhead, paler haze at the horizon, flat white clouds and graded turf
- midday is a real blue sky with a full sun over it
- grass is a green seamless turf instead of a dark biome colour over dried mud
- the ground grain is a flat grey sheet and stops smearing into streaks at grazing angles
- steadier and cheaper: the near plane leaves the depth buffer room, a block's building rows cannot grow into one another, and the shadow map runs on its own clock
- underground draws a far tighter ring and spawns cave biome enemies
- footsteps, digging and building use the game's own cc0 step-sound library, credited in the credits
- every voxel surface moved to img/textures/voxels as its own 48x48 tile
- sandbox gained a 3d planet walk into any alien biome
- scatter sprites are planted a little into the ground

- added a tools picker for choosing which sprites are displayed in each biome

# space and alien worlds
- the landing site picker offers a liminal walk as well as setting the ship down, opening the whole 3d world on the planet itself
- all forty alien biomes are written down by hand, rock and dust on the barren worlds, trees and flowers only where they belong, with the planet's own species roaming
- the ground of an alien walk is raised out of the elevation field the landing picture was painted from, sea only on worlds that have one, craters on a cratered plain
- an alien walk wraps longitude round the planet, folds latitude over the poles and lands on the exact square picked
- an alien sky is that world's own: its star's spectral type colours the daylight and what the air scatters it into
- day and night tints run on the planet's rotation, so a six hour day gets four dawns and a tidally locked world never moves off its hour
- the clock itself still keeps earth time everywhere for hunger, sleep and the calendar
- each world pulls at its own gravity, mass over radius squared, listed in the galaxy view with day length and tidal lock
- another world has its own moons in the sky, each its own size, colour and month, its star drawn the size it looks from that orbit, with its own minimap chart
- walking out of an alien world puts the party back aboard the ship in orbit, with no parked camper following
- earth gets three moons on a friday, moon 2 and moon 3, gone every other day of the week
- a ship left orbiting moon 2 or moon 3 when the week turns is put back into orbit around earth
- the moon over the 3d world is a disc lit the way it really is that day, off the same lunar arithmetic the battle sky uses
- a starship flown out of the atmosphere closes the drive, puts the party in its own interior and leaves the ship in orbit
- counted how many galaxies and single planets the galaxy system generates and wrote the results in docs
